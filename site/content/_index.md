When a USMCC paper comes out, it deserves more than a link. This makes the
post: a carousel of slides in the collaboration's look, and the words for X,
Bluesky, LinkedIn and the website.

## How it works

1. **Paste the paper's row** from the submission sheet (or just its arXiv
   link). The title, authors, summary and link fill in, with any LaTeX
   turned into readable text.
2. **Make slides.** A cover, a plain-language summary and a closing slide
   with a QR code to the paper appear in the current theme, with the USMCC
   logo.
3. **Pull in pictures.** Your photos, plots (PNG, SVG, PDF), pages of the
   paper's PDF, or its figures straight from arXiv, each with its caption
   from the paper. Photos fill a slide; plots sit on a white card.
4. **Edit.** Move, resize and turn anything; crop and zoom a picture to the
   part of the plot that matters; adjust brightness, contrast and colour; add
   text and style it. A contrast check tells you when text gets hard to read.
5. **Export.** JPEGs or PNGs for Instagram, X and Bluesky, or one PDF for a
   LinkedIn document post. On a phone, **Save Images** puts them in Photos,
   in order, ready to post.

The **Posts** tool drafts the X, Bluesky and LinkedIn posts, a
plain-language summary and the website's `index.md` from the paper. In the
desktop app, Claude can write them instead, with your API key kept in the
system keychain.

## On a phone, or a computer

The [browser version](lite/) is the same app with nothing to install. On an
iPhone or Android phone, open it and choose **Add to Home Screen** to keep it
like an app; it works offline once opened. Your carousels and pictures stay
on the device: nothing is uploaded.

The desktop app adds a few things that need a computer: exporting into a
folder, fetching from arXiv through your computer's network settings, and
writing the posts with Claude.

## Keys

On a computer, every tool has a key, and <kbd>?</kbd> lists them all.

<dl class="keys">
  <dt><kbd>A</kbd></dt><dd>add photos, PDFs and plots (or drop them anywhere)</dd>
  <dt><kbd>P</kbd></dt><dd>the paper: paste its row or arXiv link, get figures and PDF pages</dd>
  <dt><kbd>T</kbd> <kbd>L</kbd></dt><dd>add text, add the logo</dd>
  <dt><kbd>C</kbd></dt><dd>crop the selected picture (scroll to zoom)</dd>
  <dt><kbd>[</kbd> <kbd>]</kbd></dt><dd>previous or next slide</dd>
  <dt><kbd>Y</kbd></dt><dd>theme and slide size (4:5, square or story)</dd>
  <dt><kbd>W</kbd></dt><dd>write the posts</dd>
  <dt><kbd>E</kbd></dt><dd>export</dd>
</dl>
